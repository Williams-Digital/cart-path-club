Notes — pushes are sent via Expo's exp.host/--/api/v2/push/send service, which routes through Apple's APNs (iOS) and Google's FCM (Android). The app icon, "CartPath" name, and notification sound come from the app's signed entitlements; the title + body come from our chat / booking Lambdas. Reaction pushes use lower priority and a default sound. @mention and booking pushes use high priority. Time Sensitive eligibility on iOS depends on the user's Focus settings.
