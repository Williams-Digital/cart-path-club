Profit Share Model

Annual income projection by partner. Adjust the assumptions below — every number recalculates instantly.

How the split works: Gross revenue (entry fees + dues + booking fees) is net of any direct passthroughs (Stripe fees, athlete payouts). From that net, 50% goes to the CartPath operating account for expenses (AWS, software, marketing, future hires). The remaining 50% is the partner pool: Mayo (CEO) takes 35% of gross net, Dylan (CTO) takes 15% of gross net.
