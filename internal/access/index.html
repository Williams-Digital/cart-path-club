Access & Permissions Reference

Who can do what · internal use only
Confidential — Mayo & Dylan

Everyone signs in at the same screen (cartpathclub.com/app/login). After login the app automatically routes each person to the right place based on their account type. This page is the source of truth for what each type can — and can't — do. Permissions are enforced on the server, not just hidden in the app.

The five account types

Admin
You & Mayo
Runs the club. Approves applications, manages the roster, quotes & manages every booking, sees revenue and at-risk members.
Lands on /admin
Member
Paying members
Requests rounds with athletes, follows live scores, logs their own rounds, uses the clubhouse chat, manages their billing.
Lands on /members
Athlete
Pros & coaches
Sets their availability, accepts/declines round requests, logs scores, sees their earnings, uses the athlete clubhouse.
Lands on /athletes/portal
Member Assistant
A member's EA
Acts for a member: requests rounds and tracks their bookings. Never sees the member's payment method or personal billing.
Lands on /assistant
Athlete Assistant
An athlete's EA
Acts for an athlete: manages their availability calendar and views booked rounds. Can't accept/decline or submit scores.
Lands on /assistant
How assistants are created: a member or athlete invites them by email from their own portal. The assistant gets a link, sets their own password, and from then on has a separate login — they never use the member's/athlete's credentials, and every action they take is tied to their own account.

Things worth knowing

  • Assistants never have the keys. A member assistant can request rounds but can never see the member's payment method, personal profile, or billing. An athlete assistant can manage the calendar but can never accept/decline rounds, submit scores, or see earnings.
  • Everything is logged to the person. Because assistants have their own logins, every action is attributable — you always know whether the member or their assistant did something.
  • Tiers matter for members. Executive members see general channels; Chairman/Founders members also get the chairman-only channels. The app enforces this on the server.
  • Admin is locked to a short list. Only the approved admin emails (you & Mayo) can reach the admin dashboard or admin API — a regular member or athlete who somehow opened /admin would be turned away.
  • Big quotes bounce back to the member. If a member assistant requests a round above the member's approval threshold, the member has to approve the price before it proceeds.
